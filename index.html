About Me

I'm a Guatemalan transportation engineer pursuing an M.S. in Transportation Engineering at UCLA. I earned my B.S. in Industrial Engineering and Operations Research at UC Berkeley.

At UCLA's HUMAN Lab, I work with Professor Youngseo Kim. My current research uses optimization and simulation to model risk and assign tasks to autonomous drones. I’m also interested in how transit operators can plan service and route vehicles under uncertain demand.

My younger brother inspires my advocacy for autism awareness. As a graduate intern with UCLA College Corps, I mentor undergraduates one-on-one as they navigate college and plan for life after graduation.

Research, Projects & Service

Research

HUMAN Lab at UCLA, supervised by Prof. Youngseo Kim

  • Risk-Constrained Drone Routing models how to reassign tasks and routes as conditions change.
  • Spectral CVRP tests whether graph features and learned edge priorities can improve starting routes for vehicle-routing solvers.
  • Multimodal Rider Preferences uses a planned stated-choice study and multinomial logit model to examine when riders would use a shared scooter between transit legs.

Industrial Engineering & Operations Research at UC Berkeley, supervised by Prof. Chiwei Yan

CVRP Heuristics vs Machine Learning Benchmarking · August 2025–May 2026
Built a benchmark for comparing classical heuristics, metaheuristics, and machine learning methods for capacitated vehicle routing using common metrics for solution quality and runtime.

Calyber: A Ridesharing Game · May 2024–May 2025
Developed pricing and matching algorithms for shared rides and turned the research into teaching modules for incoming transfer students. The project was runner-up in the 2025 INFORMS Case Competition. I am acknowledged in the published Calyber Case Article.

CITRIS & the Banatao Institute at UC Berkeley, supervised by Dr. Jonathan Lee

Cooperative Congestion Management · May–August 2025
Assessed adoption potential and modeled fuel savings, emissions reductions, and insurance costs for congestion management across freight, transit, and insurance.
