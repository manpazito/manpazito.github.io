California College-Going & CEP Data Analysis

As a Data Management Fellow at UC Berkeley's Center for Educational Partnerships, I supported the launch and adoption of a Salesforce Education Cloud-based Data Management System for outreach programs serving California students.

  • Helped centralize student records from 12 outreach programs into a unified platform.
  • Conducted data quality checks and audits for accuracy, consistency, FERPA awareness, and security standards.
  • Built and maintained Salesforce reports and dashboards tracking college-access milestones, including applications and financial-aid records.
  • Analyzed California high-school college-going rates to compare counties, districts, and CEP partner-school patterns.

This work connected technical data management with educational access, program evaluation, and the practical challenge of making complex student-support data usable for staff and decision-makers.
