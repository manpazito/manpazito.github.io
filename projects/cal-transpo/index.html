Cal Transpo Operations & Outreach

Through the Institute of Transportation Engineers at UC Berkeley and the ASCE Transportation Team, I worked on transportation operations, multimodal planning, and outreach projects during my time at Berkeley.

  • Served as lead presenter for the MidPac 2025 transportation competition, where our team earned 1st place for transportation systems, crash, and data analysis.
  • Performed geospatial and network analysis in Python to evaluate signal timing, corridor redesigns, multimodal street layouts, and added bicycle infrastructure.
  • Co-developed outreach materials and a fictional town map to teach students multimodal transportation concepts.
  • Built Python learning materials for team members covering data analytics, simulation, and transportation-focused analysis workflows.
