Calyber: A Ridesharing Game

Calyber is a shared-ride systems project developed under Prof. Chiwei Yan at UC Berkeley. My work focused on pricing and matching algorithms for multi-pickup, multi-dropoff ride-sharing settings, as well as translating the research into teaching material for students.

  • Developed and tested heuristic search, supervised learning, and dynamic programming approaches for pricing and matching decisions.
  • Built instructional modules that introduced students to machine learning, optimization, and algorithmic decision-making through the ridesharing game.
  • Contributed to work recognized as runner-up in the 2025 INFORMS Case Competition.

The project was later adapted into a case study for algorithmic performance, scalability, and operational relevance. I am acknowledged in the published article: Yifan Shen, Chiwei Yan, Julia Yan (2026), Calyber: A Ridesharing Game, INFORMS Transactions on Education.
