Cooperative Congestion Management

This project was research support with Dr. Jonathan Lee and Prof. Alexandre Bayen at CITRIS and the Banatao Institute. I worked on the commercialization and deployment case for congestion-reduction systems, especially across freight, insurance, and transit-agency contexts.

  • Conducted market research on adoption potential, sector-specific value, and likely deployment pathways.
  • Built ROI models for fuel savings, emissions reductions, and insurance-premium impacts.
  • Assisted with technical proposals and funding materials to support pilot deployments and stakeholder engagement.

The work strengthened my interest in transportation research that combines technical modeling with institutional feasibility: a method is more useful when it can survive real budgets, incentives, and operational constraints.
