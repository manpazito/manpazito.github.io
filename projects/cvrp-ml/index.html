CVRP Heuristics vs Machine Learning Benchmarking

This UC Berkeley IEOR research project, supervised by Prof. Chiwei Yan, compared classical heuristics, metaheuristics, and machine-learning approaches for the Capacitated Vehicle Routing Problem (CVRP). The work was supported by the Marco Antonio Firebaugh Scholars Program.

  • Developed a modular benchmarking framework across diverse CVRP instance sets.
  • Implemented constructive heuristics, local search methods, and metaheuristic solvers with standardized metrics for cost, runtime, and solution quality.
  • Identified tradeoffs between solution quality and computational cost, including where ML-based approaches scale well and where training overhead becomes important.
  • Proposed a hybrid "Smarter Ants" framework combining ant colony optimization with dynamic problem decomposition through virtual depots.

The project sharpened my interest in routing research that treats algorithmic performance as a practical systems question: not just whether a method can find good routes, but when it is worth the computational complexity.
