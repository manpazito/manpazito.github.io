Risk-Constrained Dynamic Task Allocation and Routing

I’m developing a mixed-integer model that assigns tasks and routes to autonomous drones. It replans when conditions change and accounts for battery reserves, deadlines, the probability of completing a task, and limits on collision risk. I evaluate task completion, energy use, and replanning cost in synthetic simulations.

Spectral CVRP: Better First Guesses and Learned Edge Priors

Evaluating whether spectral features and learned edge priorities can improve starting routes for capacitated vehicle routing. Comparisons use the same downstream solver, matched time budgets, and independent checks of route feasibility.
More info...

Multimodal Rider Preferences for Scooter Transfers

Designing a stated-choice study to estimate how much travel time riders need to save to use a shared scooter between two transit legs. The study will use a multinomial logit (MNL) model to estimate how scooter fares and the consequences of missing a connection affect that tradeoff, while holding first- and last-mile access fixed.
