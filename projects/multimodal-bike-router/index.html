Multi-modal Bike Router For First-/Last-Mile Transit Access

This Python prototype compares bicycle-to-bus trips in Los Angeles. Each itinerary combines a bike ride to a stop, one scheduled bus ride, and a bike ride from the destination stop. The planner returns the earliest arrival and an alternative that prioritizes lower cycling exposure within a configurable delay.

How the router works

The router uses OpenStreetMap and Los Angeles bikeway data to model cycling access and egress, then checks GTFS schedules and boarding rules to find direct buses a rider can catch. Fast minimizes final arrival time. Safe favors bike routes with lower modeled exposure among itineraries that arrive within the allowed delay.

North Hollywood to UCLA

For an 8:00 AM departure on June 24, 2026, both itineraries used LA Metro bus 761. Fast took 85.4 minutes and Safe took 91.9 minutes. The Safe itinerary added 0.09 bicycle miles while increasing the share on Class I paths and Class IV protected bikeways from 14.7% to 33.4% and reducing modeled cycling exposure by 54%.

Prototype scope

The prototype uses saved schedules and supports a single direct bus ride with cycling at both ends. The exposure score reflects configurable weights for street and bikeway types. It lets riders compare modeled route conditions.
