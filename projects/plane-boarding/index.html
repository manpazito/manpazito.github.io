Stochastic Modeling of Airline Cabin Boarding Efficiency

This project developed a stochastic cabin boarding simulator in Python to evaluate boarding strategies across multiple aircraft configurations under realistic passenger behavior. It combines Monte Carlo simulation with an agent-based representation of aisle movement, seat assignments, and overhead-bin interactions.

Developed for INDENG 174: Simulation for Enterprise-Scale Systems, University of California, Berkeley (Fall 2025).

  • Modeled boarding as a discrete-time simulation with aisle blocking, passenger sequencing, seat timing, and bin-use delays.
  • Benchmarked boarding policies using total boarding duration, congestion, and passenger-level timing metrics.
  • Designed the simulator to support reproducible scenario experiments and sensitivity analysis.
